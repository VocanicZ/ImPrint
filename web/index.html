Imprinted: invisible, print/camera-robust image codes

Makes one A4 page with your picture encoded at strengths 0.75, 1, 1.5 and 2 (the default picks ~0.8 for busy pictures, ~1.2 for smooth ones), labelled with plain text. Print it at 100% / actual size, check which ones you can see, then photograph the page and decode it: each decoded copy reports its strength (payload d1.5…).
